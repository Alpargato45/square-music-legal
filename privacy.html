Privacy Policy — Square Music Uploader

Last updated: November 16, 2026.

Square Music Uploader is used only by the owner of the YouTube channel MarbleMusic45Dev to manage that channel.

Data accessed

With the channel owner's authorization (Google OAuth), the app accesses the owner's YouTube channel to upload and schedule videos, edit video details, read channel and video statistics (YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API) and post or reply to comments on the channel's own videos.

Use and storage

The data is used only to provide these functions to the channel owner. Authorization tokens and statistics are stored locally on the owner's computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
